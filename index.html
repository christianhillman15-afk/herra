What we do

Full-service craftsmanship, one trusted crew

From a single backsplash to a full backyard hardscape, Herrera's Tile handles the details most contractors overlook. Here's how we can help.

Tile Installation

Floors, showers, backsplashes, and feature walls. Perfectly aligned layouts, waterproof substrates, and grout lines that stay crisp for years.

  • Porcelain, ceramic & natural stone
  • Custom showers & wet rooms
  • Backsplashes & accent walls

Paver Installation

Patios, walkways, driveways, and pool decks built on properly compacted bases so they stay level and beautiful through every Arizona summer.

  • Patios & outdoor living spaces
  • Driveways & walkways
  • Pool decks & retaining walls

Handyman Services

The reliable pro for the growing to-do list. Repairs, installs, and finishing touches done cleanly, on time, and done once.

  • Repairs & general maintenance
  • Fixture & hardware installs
  • Punch lists & touch-ups

Framing

Straight, square, and code-conscious framing for remodels and additions — the solid foundation every great finish is built on.

  • Walls & partitions
  • Remodels & additions
  • Structural & finish framing
